# PhenomeBeauty Product Pricing Strategy

**Status:** Proposed working price architecture
**Prepared:** 11 November 2026
**Scope:** Intimate Collection price anchor and Restore & Refine Body Collection launch pricing
**Currency:** South African rand (ZAR)

## 1. Pricing objective

Establish a clear product price hierarchy that protects PhenomeBeauty's premium positioning while making the Restore & Refine Body Collection a slightly more accessible choice.

The Intimate Collection remains the premium price anchor. Its higher pricing reflects its specialised formulation and positioning. The Body Collection is intended for broader, general body-care use and may sit slightly below the Intimate Collection because of its formulation scope.

This is a difference in product purpose and formulation, not a statement that the Body Collection is lower quality.

The Body Collection should feel like considered premium value, not a budget alternative.

## 4. Price architecture

| Tier | Restore cream | Refine scrub | Ritual kit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intimate Collection | R260 | R220 | R440 |
| Restore & Refine Body Collection | R239 | R199 | R399 |

This structure creates a deliberate hierarchy:

1. **Intimate Collection:** premium anchor, supported by specialised formulation.
2. **Restore & Refine Body Collection:** slightly more accessible, broad-use body care, while retaining premium value cues.
3. **Ritual bundles:** encourage multi-product purchases and provide a clear saving without undermining standalone product prices.

The price gaps are intentionally controlled rather than large. The Body Collection should not be framed as a cheaper or inferior version of the Intimate Collection.

## 5. Psychological pricing rationale

The recommended prices use just-below or charm pricing:

- R199 sits just below R200.
- R239 sits just below R240.
- R399 sits just below R400.

These price endings can make the purchase feel more accessible at a glance while preserving the intended premium range.

The effect should support the positioning, not replace the need for strong product quality, formulation, packaging and brand communication.

## 6. Reverse-pricing and cost guardrails

The retail prices are being considered first. Formulation and manufacturing costs should then be worked backwards against the price ceiling, rather than allowing production costs to dictate a retail price that weakens the intended positioning.

For an initial planning scenario, use a 65% gross margin target as a working assumption. This is a planning benchmark, not a confirmed PhenomeBeauty margin or a final financial approval.

At a simple 65% gross margin calculation:

| Product / offer | Proposed retail price | Maximum direct cost at 35% |
|---|---|---|
| Body Restore Moisturising Cream, 250ml | R239 | R83.65 |
| Body Refine Exfoliating Scrub, 250ml | R199 | R69.65 |
| Body Ritual bundle | R399 | R139.65 |

The bundle ceiling is the maximum combined direct cost for the complete bundle. It must include the direct costs of both products and any included packaging or components. Do not count the same packaging or assembly cost twice.

### Important VAT and margin qualification

These ceilings are indicative only. If PhenomeBeauty is VAT registered and the displayed retail prices include VAT, gross margin must be calculated using revenue excluding VAT.

The VAT treatment of the selling price and input costs must be confirmed before approving formulation budgets or supplier quotes.

The direct-cost model should also explicitly identify whether it includes:

- Ingredients and formulation inputs
- Manufacturing or filling
- Primary containers and closures
- Labels and printed materials
- Secondary or gift packaging
- Direct assembly costs

Payment fees, fulfilment, shipping subsidies, marketing, overhead and other operating expenses should be modelled separately when assessing contribution and net profitability.

## 7. Decisions still required before launch approval

The prices in this document are proposed working prices, not approved live catalogue prices.

Before final sign-off:

1. Confirm PhenomeBeauty's VAT registration status and whether shelf prices are VAT-inclusive.
2. Obtain final formulation and manufacturing quotes for each 250ml product.
3. Confirm packaging specifications and costs.
4. Confirm the exact Body Ritual bundle contents and all associated costs.
5. Calculate gross margin using the correct VAT basis.
6. Model contribution after payment fees, fulfilment and marketing.
7. Review the final price points against customer response and actual sales performance.

If the direct costs cannot support the target margin at these prices, revisit the formulation, packaging, supplier terms or bundle structure before automatically increasing retail prices.

Any price change should preserve the relationship between the two collections.

## 8. Working recommendation

Proceed with the following as the pricing hypothesis for costing and launch planning:

- Body Restore Moisturising Cream, 250ml: R239
- Body Refine Exfoliating Scrub, 250ml: R199
- Restore & Refine Body Ritual: R399

Retain the Intimate Collection at its current reference prices of R260, R220 and R440 respectively while costing and validating the Body Collection.
