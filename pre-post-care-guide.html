Before andafter your wax.

Simple care for smoother skin and calmer results between visits.

Download as PDF

Before

  • Don’t shave. Hair should be about 0.5 to 1cm, roughly a grain of rice.
  • Keep the area clean. No lotion, oil, deodorant or makeup.
  • Exfoliate gently 24 hours before. It lifts dead skin and helps hair release.
  • Avoid caffeine and alcohol. They can make skin more sensitive.
  • Wear loose clothing to your appointment, for comfort afterwards.
  • If you’re nervous, a mild pain reliever 30 minutes before is an option. Check with your doctor if unsure.

After

  • For 24 hours, skip sex, the gym, swimming, and hot showers or saunas. It lowers irritation and infection risk.
  • Soothe the skin with aloe gel or a post-wax product.
  • Exfoliate two to three times a week, from 48 hours after. It helps prevent ingrowns.
  • Moisturise daily. Soft skin helps hair grow through more easily.
  • Avoid sun and sunbeds for at least 48 hours.
  • Don’t tweeze or shave between visits. Stick to your wax cycle for the best results.
