What is Tara-S?

Tara-S is a free period tracker, built by Phenomebeauty for South African women.

It follows your cycle, your symptoms and your skin, so you can notice your own patterns.

The name comes from Khoekhoegowab, a language of Southern Africa.

Women have always known their rhythms. Tara-S helps you remember yours.
