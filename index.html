Your First Step

Meet the
Smooth Start.

If you are new to PhenomeBeauty, or simply unsure about waxing, this is where we recommend you begin.

The Smooth Start is designed to bring you into the PhenomeBeauty world without expecting you to already know what to do, what to ask or what to book.

We talk you through the treatment, help you understand your skin and your routine, and make the experience feel much less mysterious. There is no judgement and no pressure to know everything beforehand.

  • A comfortable introduction to waxing
  • Clear guidance before, during and after
  • No judgement about your body or experience
  • A simple way into your ongoing beauty routine

No Surprises

So, what actually happens?

If you have been putting off waxing because you do not know what the appointment is going to be like, you are not alone. Here is the simple version.

01

We arrive at your door.

No salon waiting room. Your appointment happens in your own space, which can make the whole experience feel a little easier.

02

We talk first.

You can ask questions. Tell us if you are nervous. Tell us if this is your first time. Knowing what is happening makes a difference.

03

Then we get started.

Your therapist prepares the area and works methodically, explaining anything you need to know along the way.

04

You leave knowing what comes next.

Good beauty care should not end when the appointment ends. We help you understand aftercare and how to look after your skin.
