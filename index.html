The thinking behind Tara-S

Your body isn't a formula.

Women do not all move through the same cycle on the same schedule. Tara-S was created to make room for that difference.

It gives women a place to track their own rhythm, understand what they are experiencing and explore information when they want it.

The app can also point you towards your ideal beauty window when the timing is right. That reminder is there if you want it. It can be dismissed and will not return until the next month.

Tara-S is available to everyone, not only PhenomeBeauty clients. It exists because the idea is useful on its own.
