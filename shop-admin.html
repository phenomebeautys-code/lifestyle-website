Product feeds

Your products for Google Shopping. It updates itself from your product list.

Google Merchant Center
  1. In Google Merchant Center, go to Products, then Add products, then Add from a file, and choose to add a file link.
  2. Paste the feed link above and set it to fetch daily.
  3. Set up shipping for South Africa in Merchant Center (Shipping and returns). Use your Pudo rates, and your free-delivery amount if it is on.
