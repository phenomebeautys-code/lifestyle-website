Waxing services we offer

Our mobile waxing menu covers everything from quick touch-ups to full transformations, with meticulous hygiene and technique.

  • Brazilian & Hollywood waxing
  • Bikini line and intimate waxing
  • Leg, arm and underarm waxing
  • Facial waxing (brows, lip, chin)
  • Full body waxing combinations

Treatments are performed by experienced technicians using high-quality wax and aftercare, tailored to your skin type and comfort level.

Areas we cover

Phenomebeauty is a mobile service, travelling across Cape Town so you can enjoy waxing where you feel most at ease. We regularly visit suburbs including:

  • City Bowl (CBD, Gardens, Vredehoek, Oranjezicht)
  • Atlantic Seaboard (Sea Point, Green Point, Mouille Point)
  • Southern Suburbs (Claremont, Rondebosch, Newlands, Kenilworth)
  • Woodstock & Observatory

If you’re just outside these areas, reach out and we’ll confirm availability and travel options.

Why choose mobile waxing?

Mobile waxing lets you skip traffic, parking and waiting rooms. You choose the time, we bring the salon experience to you.

  • Private, familiar environment
  • Flexible appointment times
  • No travel or parking stress
  • Consistent, personalised care from the same specialist team
